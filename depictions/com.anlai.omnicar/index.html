OmniCar

The power of CarPlay.

Split Screen

  • Two or three CarPlay apps side by side, each with its real CarPlay interface
  • 2 panes, 3 panes or 1 large + 2; drag the divider, swap panes, floating window
  • Three favourite layouts; Siri and Shortcuts through omnicar://

App Bridge

  • iPhone apps without CarPlay (YouTube, TikTok…) on the car screen, no CarBridge needed

Speed Bubble

  • Current speed and the posted limit from Vietmap Live, GOFA, Waze or Wyn, 9 styles, warnings with sound

Startup Screen

  • Your own video every time CarPlay connects, or a Weather / Briefing card
